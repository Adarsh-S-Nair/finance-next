Privacy policy

This policy describes Short Studio, the private video-production application operated for The Unquiet Chapter, and this public information website at studio.zervo.app.

Who operates the application

The operator of The Unquiet Chapter uses Short Studio to produce and manage the channel’s videos. For privacy questions or requests, contact theunquietchapter@gmail.com. This is a separate service from the finance application hosted elsewhere on zervo.app.

Information the private application accesses

When the operator connects a YouTube account through Google OAuth, Short Studio receives authorization tokens. It does not receive the account’s Google password. Its permissions allow video uploads and read access to YouTube account information.

The application accesses the connected channel’s identifier, name and statistics, and video identifiers, titles, publication dates, privacy and upload status, and available view, like and comment counts. It also stores production information such as topics, research, scripts, generated images, narration, rendered videos, job timestamps, settings and upload records.

How information is used

Authorization tokens are used to make authorized YouTube API requests. Channel and video information is used to confirm the destination channel, display uploads and publication status, show raw statistics, and maintain upload records. Production files are used to create, render and upload videos.

Short Studio does not sell personal information or YouTube API data. The public website does not offer account registration, accept payments, or display advertising.

Service providers and sharing

Short Studio uses YouTube API Services. When it uploads a video, Google receives the video and its accompanying metadata. Google’s handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The private application runs on a Hostinger-hosted server, accessed through a private Tailscale network. AI providers, including OpenAI, process prompts and production content used for research, scripts and illustrations; YouTube performance-derived topic scoring is disabled, and new topic-generation prompts do not use prior channel statistics. Vercel hosts this public website. Providers process information needed to deliver their respective services, subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Storage and security

Production files, authorization credentials and saved performance records are stored on the private application server, not on this public website. Access is restricted to the operator’s private environment. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Production files remain available for the operator’s workflow unless removed. Channel and video metadata are refreshed or removed within 30 days; saved statistical snapshots expire after 30 days. The application runs background authorization and data-maintenance checks, normally hourly when idle, and may refresh more frequently while the dashboard is open. A confirmed expired or revoked Google authorization starts deletion of the application’s stored YouTube data. Failed cleanup operations hold YouTube access for retry and require the operator to resolve persistent failures.

Revoking access and requesting deletion

The private application’s Studio and Office analytics panels provide a “Disconnect YouTube & delete saved YouTube data” control. After explicit confirmation, it revokes the Google authorization, deletes the active server credential, and clears cached channel data, saved statistics and remote upload links. A local marker without YouTube identifiers prevents accidental duplicate uploads. Scripts, images, narration and rendered videos are not deleted by that control. You can also revoke access through Google’s account permissions page. To request deletion of other application records or development copies, email theunquietchapter@gmail.com and identify the connected channel and data concerned; the operator handles these requests manually.

Deleting data from Short Studio does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube. Those videos can be managed separately in YouTube Studio. The operator may need to verify that a request comes from the account owner.

Website visits and device storage

This public website sets no application cookies, uses no analytics scripts and embeds no YouTube player. Following its YouTube links opens YouTube, whose policies then apply. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser information, to serve and secure the website. The private application may use browser storage for preferences such as the selected theme.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the application changes; the revision date is shown above. Send questions, correction requests or privacy concerns to theunquietchapter@gmail.com.
