BEHIND THE CHANNEL

One workspace, from idea to upload.

Short Studio helps the channel operator research topics, write scripts, create illustrations and narration, and assemble captioned videos. AI-assisted production is part of that process.

With the operator’s Google authorization, it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the connected channel and retrieve channel and video information, publication status, and performance statistics.

This website is a public introduction—not the production dashboard. The application is private and is not open for public sign-up. It is independent of Google and YouTube and is not affiliated with them.
